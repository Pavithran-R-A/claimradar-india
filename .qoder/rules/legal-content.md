# Legal & Content Policy

## Platform Positioning

- ClaimRadar India is an **information service**, not a law firm, consultancy or government body.
- We do **not** guarantee payouts, represent claimants, or handle compensation money.
- Every public page carries a disclaimer: _"This is an informational service. Not legal advice. Verify independently before acting."_

## Data Integrity

- Demo / seed data must be clearly labelled as **DEMO — NOT REAL** in both UI and API responses.
- Never present a real company name alongside invented allegations or financial figures.
- If a real company is referenced, the underlying claim must trace to an official public source document.

## Publication Rules

| Status                | Auto-publish?       | Human review                    |
| --------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| `official_update`     | Yes                 | Optional spot-check             |
| `potential_claimable` | Yes                 | Recommended                     |
| `verified_claimable`  | **Never initially** | Mandatory editor + legal review |

- A claim reaches `verified_claimable` only after: (1) ≥ 2 independent official sources, (2) deterministic validation passes, (3) editor sign-off, (4) legal-reviewer sign-off.

## Trademark & IP

- "ClaimRadar India" is a **temporary** project name — IP India trademark search required before commercial launch.
- Third-party trademarks (company names, logos) used only for factual identification, never implying endorsement.

## Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] Legal counsel reviews all public-facing copy and disclaimers.
- [ ] IP India trademark search completed for final brand name.
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms of service published.
- [ ] Data-protection impact assessment completed (DPDP Act 2023 compliance).

## Content Corrections

- Every correction creates a new revision row; the original remains in audit history.
- Public pages show "Last verified: <date>" and link to the correction log where applicable.
