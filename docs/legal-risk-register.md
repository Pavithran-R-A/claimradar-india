# Legal Risk Register

## Key Risks & Mitigations

### Defamation

- **Risk**: Publishing inaccurate claims about a company's conduct or financial penalties.
- **Mitigation**: Every `verified_claimable` claim requires ≥ 2 official sources + editor + legal sign-off. Corrections workflow in place. Language is factual and sourced, never speculative.
- **Residual**: Medium — mitigated further by professional indemnity insurance before commercial launch.

### Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023)

- **Risk**: Processing personal data without consent or lawful basis.
- **Mitigation**: We only process data from official public documents. User account data collected minimally (email, preferences). Privacy policy published. Data Protection Officer appointed before launch.
- **Residual**: Low for public-source data; Medium for user accounts — mitigated by consent-based collection.

### Trademark Infringement

- **Risk**: Using third-party trademarks (company names, logos) without authorisation.
- **Mitigation**: Used only for factual identification (nominative fair use). No implication of endorsement. "ClaimRadar India" name requires IP India search before commercial launch.
- **Residual**: Low — standard nominative use.

### Regulatory (Financial Advice)

- **Risk**: Being classified as providing financial or legal advice.
- **Mitigation**: Clear disclaimers on every page: "Informational service, not legal advice. Verify independently." We never handle compensation money or represent claimants. Terms of service explicitly disclaim advisory role.
- **Residual**: Medium — mitigated by legal counsel review of all public copy before launch.

### Financial (Payment Handling)

- **Risk**: Being classified as a payment intermediary or money transmitter.
- **Mitigation**: We never hold, transfer or disburse compensation funds. Razorpay handles subscription billing only. Clear contractual separation.
- **Residual**: Very Low.

### Source Access & Copyright

- **Risk**: Copyright claims from source publishers.
- **Mitigation**: We extract facts (not copyrightable), not verbatim text. Summaries are original. Raw documents stored internally for verification, not republished.
- **Residual**: Low.

### Platform Misuse

- **Risk**: Users relying on platform data for legal action without independent verification.
- **Mitigation**: Prominent disclaimers. "Last verified" dates on every claim. Encouragement to seek professional advice.
- **Residual**: Medium — inherent to the domain.

## Pre-Launch Legal Checklist

- [ ] Legal counsel reviews all public-facing copy.
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance secured.
- [ ] DPDP Act compliance audit completed.
- [ ] IP India trademark search for final brand name.
- [ ] Terms of service and privacy policy published.
